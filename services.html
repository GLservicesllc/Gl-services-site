Services

Everything we handle for property managers, turn by turn. Not seeing what you need? Ask — if it's maintenance-related, there's a good chance we can help.

General Repairs

The everyday stuff that piles up between service calls: loose cabinet hinges, sticking doors, broken blinds, towel bars, closet rods, minor appliance issues, and anything else on a routine maintenance list. We knock these out efficiently so small problems don't turn into tenant complaints.

Turnover Maintenance

Full unit turn between leases, built around the tight windows of the student housing calendar. Repairs, wall touch-ups, fixture checks, and cleanup so the unit is ready to list and show with no delay between move-out and move-in.

Drywall & Painting

Patch holes and dents from move-outs, texture matching, and full repaints — single rooms or entire units. We match existing paint where possible to keep costs down and avoid repainting whole units unnecessarily.

Plumbing Fixes

Leaky faucets, running or clogged toilets, slow drains, loose fixtures, and other non-emergency plumbing issues. For anything beyond standard fixes (major line breaks, water heater replacement, etc.), we'll tell you straight up if it needs a licensed plumber.
