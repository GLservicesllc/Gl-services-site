How does billing work?

You're billed for time on-site, rounded to the nearest quarter hour. A two-technician job (like moving furniture, hanging drywall, or anything requiring an extra set of hands) bills at the two-technician rate for the full visit.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Call, text, or fill out the contact form and we'll give you a free estimate before any work begins.

Are materials included?

Labor rates cover our time. Materials (paint, parts, replacement fixtures, etc.) are billed at cost plus 10%, and we'll flag anything significant before buying it so there are no surprises on your invoice.

Is there a minimum charge?

Small jobs are billed for actual time worked — we don't pad invoices with inflated minimums. For exact numbers on a specific job, just ask when you request a quote.
